# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep participation in this project welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.
We act with honesty, patience, and respect in every interaction.

## Expected behaviour

Be considerate toward other contributors and their time.
Give actionable feedback tied to the owning specification or standard.
Assume good intent, and ask before attributing motive.
Keep discussion focused on the change under review.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insulting remarks, and personal attacks have no place here.
Unwelcome advances, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are equally out of scope.
Publishing private details without consent is forbidden.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately through the path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers review each report confidentially and respond with a proportionate remedy.
Remedies range from a warning through temporary or permanent exclusion.
Decisions aim to protect participants while remaining fair to all sides.

## Scope

This code applies across issues, pull requests, discussions, and related project spaces.
It also covers representing the project in public forums.
Contributors accept these terms by taking part.
